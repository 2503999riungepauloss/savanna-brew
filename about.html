Our story

From the highlands to your cup.

Savanna Brew Café opened in 2019 with one small espresso machine and a big dream: to celebrate Kenyan coffee. Founder Amina Otieno grew up on her grandparents' coffee farm in Nyeri and wanted to bring that flavour to the city.

Today the café supports local farmers, uses eco-friendly packaging and hosts community events every month. We believe a great café is more than a place to get coffee — it is a place to feel at home.

“I wanted to create a space where people could experience the beauty of Kenyan coffee and feel part of a community, one cup at a time.”— Amina Otieno, Founder
